Smart tools

How AI helps you care for street animals

Turn photos into draft reports, search reports in your own words and translate community updates. Feeding records also help you plan routes and follow each colony’s care. You review suggestions and decide what to do next.

Spot and report

Reporting and finding animals

Create a report from a photo

Add a photo and AI suggests details about the animal and its visible needs. Review the draft, correct anything it missed and confirm the location before posting.

Check whether an animal has already been reported

Before your report is posted, the app looks for what may be the same animal reported nearby in the last three days. Check the photo and choose whether to help on that report or post your own.

Search in your own words

Describe the animal or situation you are looking for. Search can suggest related reports even when the wording differs. Check the photos and details to confirm a match.

Plan and coordinate

Planning and coordination

Plan around colonies overdue for a recorded feed

Choose where you start, how far you can travel and how many stops you can make. Suggested routes use recorded feeding patterns to highlight colonies that may need a visit. Review and edit the stops before you go.

A missing check-in does not necessarily mean the animals have not been fed.

Help donated food reach local feeders

See food offers, collection addresses and pickup windows. Coordinate with the community so people know who is on the way and when the food has been collected.

Follow each colony’s care

See recent feeding check-ins, estimated animal numbers and recorded sterilisation updates. These records help your community plan the next visit.

Understand and share

Understanding and sharing

Two languages, one conversation.

Read Arabic and English community updates with translations alongside the original. Check important details such as names, amounts and collection times.

Turn rescue updates into a story to share

Create a draft from a report’s recorded timeline. Review the details and the people named before choosing to share it.

Screens from the app in English; the Arabic app reads the same.

Assistance with limits

You stay in control

AI suggestions can be wrong. Check report details, search results and translations before acting on them. You choose what to post and which route to take.
