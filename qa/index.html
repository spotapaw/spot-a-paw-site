Test plan

Everything in the app, in the order a real person meets it. Work top to bottom. Tick each case as you finish it; tick Problem and write what happened if it didn't do what the box says it should. Your ticks are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

Before you start

What you need

  • An iPhone with the TestFlight build installed. (Android isn't released yet.)
  • A working email address you can open on the phone — you'll need to click a verification link.
  • To be outdoors or able to fake a location for a few cases. Cairo is ideal; anywhere works.
  • About 90 minutes for a full pass. Sections 1–6 are the critical hour.

How to report

For anything that fails, tick Problem and in the box write:

  1. What you did, step by step
  2. What you expected
  3. What actually happened
  4. A screenshot or screen recording — this is the single most useful thing

Then use Print at the end to save the whole sheet as a PDF and send it back.

Known already — please don't report these. They'll waste your time and mine:

  • Android push doesn't work. Being fixed separately. iOS push should work.
  • Shared paw links open the website, not the app. That's expected for now — the page itself should work and look right, and that is worth testing.
  • There is no Facebook sign-in. It was removed deliberately.
  • Some paws around Cairo are demo data seeded for testing. They're real database rows, so they behave like real reports. Don't be alarmed by them, and don't judge "is the map busy?" by them.
  • The app is nearly empty of real users. Empty lists are usually correct, not broken. What matters is whether the empty state says something sensible.

5. Reporting an animal — Spot a Paw

The single most important thing the app does. Take your time here.

5.1

Report with a photo from the camera

  1. Tap Spot a Paw in the bottom bar.
  2. Take a photo (of anything — a cushion is fine).
  3. Choose dog or cat, pick what's needed (food, water, medical, rescue, mother & pups).
  4. Type a description.
  5. Check the location on the little map, then submit.
Expect: it saves and you're taken to the animal's page or the feed, and your new paw is on the map where you were. The description box sits on its own parchment panel and the text isn't clipped.
Must passArabic
5.2

Report with a photo from the library, and with a video

  1. Repeat 5.1 but pick an existing photo.
  2. Then repeat once more with a short video.
Expect: both upload. The video shows a still thumbnail in the feed rather than a black rectangle, and plays when tapped.
Important
5.3

Moving the pin by hand

  1. Start a report and drag the map / pin to a spot down the road.
  2. Submit and then find it on the main map.
Expect: it lands where you put it, not where you're standing.
Important
5.4

Submitting with things missing

  1. Try to submit with no photo.
  2. Try with no description.
  3. Try with location permission denied (Settings → Spot a Paw → Location → Never).
Expect: each one either explains what's missing or handles it sensibly. Nothing should hang, crash, or save a broken report. Turn location back on afterwards.
Important
5.5

A very long description

  1. Paste a long paragraph — 500 words or so — into the description and submit.
Expect: it saves, and the feed card truncates it neatly with an ellipsis rather than overflowing or pushing the layout apart.

8. Feeding spots and routes

The part a regular feeder uses every evening.

8.1

Create a feeding spot

  1. From the map or the Feeding Spots tab, add a spot: name it, say what's needed, set the location.
Expect: it saves and appears on the map and in the list.
ImportantArabic
8.2

Fed today / Seen check-ins

  1. On a spot, tap Fed today. Then Seen.
  2. Leave the page and come back.
Expect: the check-in sticks, shows who and when, and the card updates.
Important
8.3

A sensitive spot hides its exact location

  1. Create a spot and mark it sensitive.
  2. Sign in as a different, ordinary account and find that spot.
Expect: the other account sees the name and what's needed, but no exact coordinates and no predictable feeding time — an approximate area at most. This protects colonies from people who harm them, so treat any leak as serious.
Must pass
8.4

Tonight's feeding route

  1. Add two or three spots to a route and start it.
  2. Mark the first stop done.
Expect: a route bar pins above the list, stops are in order, and finishing one advances to the next.
8.5

Editing someone else's spot

  1. From a second ordinary account, try to rename or delete a spot you didn't create.
Expect: you can't. Either the option isn't offered, or it's refused clearly. It must not silently appear to work.
Important
8.6

Take back something you logged by mistake

  1. On a colony, mark Sterilised (or Microchipped).
  2. Open View history.
  3. Remove your own entry.
  4. From a SECOND account, add an entry, then go back to the first account and open the history again.
Expect: your own entry can be removed and the colony's counts go back down. The other account's entry is visible but has no remove control — you can only take back your own.
Important
8.7

Only your own toggles, and they toggle off

  1. Tap Fed today, Seen them, Sterilised, Microchipped on a colony.
  2. Tap each one again.
  3. Check the same colony from a second account.
Expect: each tap turns the button on and records it; tapping again turns it off and removes it. The second account sees its own buttons off — these are per-person, not per-colony.
Important
8.8

How far away colonies are

  1. Open Feeding Spots and change the distance chip (5, 10, 25, 50, 100 km, and everywhere).
  2. Watch the list and the distance shown on each card.
Expect: the list shortens and lengthens as you change it, and each colony shows a believable distance from where you are. Everywhere shows all of them.

Telling every member — owners only

  4. Set a window that has already passed, and look again.

14. Bad network, and edges

14.1

No connection at all

  1. Turn on aeroplane mode and open the app.
  2. Try to open the feed, and try to submit a report.
Expect: the app says it can't reach the internet. It should not hang forever on a spinner or crash. Cached artwork should still draw.
Important
14.2

Losing signal mid-upload

  1. Start submitting a report with a photo, then switch on aeroplane mode straight away.
Expect: it fails and says so. What it must not do is claim success, or leave a half-saved report with no photo.
Important
14.3

Interruptions

  1. While in the middle of a report, take a phone call or switch apps for a minute, then come back.
Expect: your half-finished report is still there.
14.4

Rotate, and large text

  1. Turn the phone sideways on the map, the feed and a report.
  2. Settings → Accessibility → Display → Larger Text, turn it well up, then reopen the app.
Expect: nothing overlaps, nothing is cut off, and no yellow-and-black overflow stripes appear. Buttons stay tappable.
