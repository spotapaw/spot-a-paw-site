Android testers wanted

Help us launch Spot a Paw on Google Play.

Before a new app can go live on Google Play, it needs real people to test it for 14 days. Two minutes of your time helps street animals get seen.

Sign up to test

How it works

  1. Sign up with your first name and the Gmail address on your Android phone.
  2. We email you a link as soon as the test opens. Open it on your phone and tap Become a tester, then install Spot a Paw from Google Play.
  3. Keep the app installed for 14 days. Use it when you see a street animal that needs help, and tell us what could be better.

What you need

  • An Android phone (iPhone testing comes later).
  • A Google account (Gmail). It has to be the one signed in on the phone.

Your privacy

Your email is only used to give you access to the test and to send you the install link. Testers can't see each other's details. You can leave the test at any time from the Google Play testing page. Questions: support@spot-a-paw.com.
